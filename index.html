Understanding Rolling Physics & Calculation Rules

1. Mandatory 600mm Flat Allowance: Commercial 3-roll section benders require leverage at the start and end of a bar. This leaves roughly $300\text{mm}$ unbent flat metal on both ends ($600\text{mm}$ total per bar). Rolling full factory lengths eliminates paying this waste allowance on individual segments!
2. Procurement OD vs Workshop Cut: Calculating flat length on the Outer Diameter ($\text{OD}$) builds in a safe geometric buffer so buyers never under-order steel. When cutting rolled arcs on the shop floor, the neutral axis ($(\text{OD}-t) \times \pi / 360$) ensures parts roll to exact radius without binding.
3. Saw Blade Kerf Deduction: Cold-sawing or angle-grinder slitting consumes $3\text{mm}$ to $5\text{mm}$ of steel width per cut. The calculator accounts for kerf loss when slitting multiple segments out of a single rolled bar.
